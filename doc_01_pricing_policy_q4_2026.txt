Author: Finance & Commercial Operations Committee

1. OBJECTIVE & SCOPE
This policy establishes mandatory pricing boundaries, margin thresholds, volume discount tiers, and customer tier guidelines for all standard and engineered fasteners produced and distributed by Apex Precision Fasteners.

2. DETERMINISTIC PRICING CONSTRAINTS (MANDATORY)
2.1 Price Floor (Loss Prevention Constraint):
Under no circumstances may any sales personnel, quotation copilot, or manager issue a quotation below the Direct Unit Cost + 9.0% mandatory floor margin. 
The Price Floor is calculated as:
  Price Floor = Direct Unit Cost * 1.09
Any request below this floor is mathematically blocked unless an explicit override with Managing Director authorization is logged.

2.2 Standard Customer Tier Margins:
Baseline target gross margins prior to volume discounts:
  - Platinum Tier: 14.0% to 16.0% target margin (Net-45 credit terms)
  - Gold Tier:     18.0% to 20.0% target margin (Net-30 credit terms)
  - Silver Tier:   22.0% to 24.0% target margin (Net-15 credit terms)
  - Bronze Tier:   26.0% to 28.0% target margin (Advance / 7-day payment)

2.3 Volume Discount Schedule:
Standard volume discounts applicable across all product categories:
  - Tier 1 (1 to 99 units):      0.0% discount
  - Tier 2 (100 to 499 units):   3.5% discount off standard list price
  - Tier 3 (500 to 999 units):   6.0% discount off standard list price
  - Tier 4 (1,000 to 4,999 units): 9.0% discount off standard list price
  - Tier 5 (5,000+ units):       12.0% discount off standard list price

2.4 Inventory and Lead Time Rules:
- If current stock level is less than requested order quantity, an expedited production/procurement fee of 2.5% to 3.5% may be applied if customer requests delivery within 10 days.
- If inventory exceeds 200% of reorder level (excess stock), sales representatives are authorized to offer an additional 2.0% clearance incentive to liquidate inventory.

3. HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP APPROVAL PROTOCOL
All automated copilot recommendations must be reviewed by the assigned Sales Executive. Any manual price deviation exceeding 3% from the copilot's recommended price requires an audit explanation note.
