Subject: Win/Loss Debrief - Deccan Fabrication Works (Customer ID: CUST-003)

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY:
In August 2026, we lost a key tender for 1,000 pieces of Heavy Hex Flange Nut Grade 10 (M16, SKU: HFN-M16-10) to a regional distributor representing Sundaram Fasteners.
Our Quote: ₹36.80 per unit (24.0% margin applied per standard Silver tier policy).
Winning Competitor Quote: ₹34.20 per unit (7.6% lower).

PURCHASE HEAD FEEDBACK:
During a follow-up meeting with Mr. S. Rao (VP Procurement, Deccan Fabrication Works):
1. Deccan highly values our in-house metallurgical testing lab and NABL-accredited test certificates, which the competitor could not supply without third-party delay.
2. Mr. Rao explicitly stated that if our price is within ₹34.80 to ₹35.40 per unit for batch sizes of 500 units or greater, Deccan will award 100% of their Q4 requirement to Apex.
3. Our unit cost on HFN-M16-10 is ₹29.50. 
   - A price of ₹34.90 yields a 15.5% gross margin (well above our mandatory 9.0% floor of ₹32.16).
   - Standard Silver tier margin is 22%, which makes us uncompetitive for this client.

RECOMMENDATION FOR COPILOT & SALES REPS:
For any forthcoming quotation for Deccan Fabrication Works on HFN-M16-10 or related heavy fasteners:
- Cap target margin between 15.0% and 17.0% rather than 22.0% Silver default.
- Mention NABL Material Test Certificate inclusion in the quote terms to reinforce quality differentiation.
